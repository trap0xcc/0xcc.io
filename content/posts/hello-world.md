## What does `0xCC` mean?

`0xCC` is the encoding for the trap instruction on amd64, aka `int3`. I've done
a bit of work on debuggers and tracers that use this instruction to interpose
arbitrary code in order to better understand it. That is my goal with everything
I do in computing: to better understand how software systems work.
